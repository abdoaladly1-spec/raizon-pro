# 🚀 50 Professional Suggestions for ProCashier System Development

Here is a comprehensive roadmap of 50 features and improvements to elevate your cashier system to a world-class standard.

## 🎨 UI/UX (User Interface & Experience)
1. **Customizable Dashboard:** Allow users to drag-and-drop widgets (charts, quick actions) on the dashboard.
2. **Touch Mode:** Create a toggle for "Touch Mode" that increases button sizes and spacing for touchscreens.
3. **Sound Effects:** Add pleasant audio feedback for successful actions, errors, and printing.
4. **Keyboard Shortcuts Map:** Pressing `?` should show a visual overlay of all available keyboard shortcuts.
5. **Multi-Window Support:** Allow popping out the Invoice screen into a separate window for multi-monitor setups.
6. **Themes Gallery:** Add more color themes (Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Midnight) beyond just Dark/Light.
7. **Compact Mode:** A dense view for invoices list to show more rows on screen.
8. **Right-Click Menus:** Add context menus to table rows (Edit, Delete, Duplicate, Print).
9. **Screen Saver:** Display shop logo or advertisements when the system is idle.
10. **Global Search (Ctrl+K):** A spotlight-style search bar to jump to any page, customer, or product instantly.

## 🧾 Invoicing & Sales
11. **Hold/Park Bill:** Save an invoice as "Pending" to serve another customer, then resume it later.
12. **Split Payments:** Allow paying part Cash and part Card for a single invoice.
13. **Installments:** Manage partial payments and track remaining balance for customers.
14. **Refund/Returns:** Dedicated workflow for returns with reason codes and inventory restock options.
15. **Gift Receipts:** Print receipts without prices for gifts.
16. **Recurring Invoices:** Auto-generate invoices for subscription-based services (e.g., Gym membership).
17. **Dynamic Service Charges:** Auto-add service fees or tips based on rules (e.g., large groups).
18. **Multi-Currency:** Accept payments in different currencies with real-time or fixed exchange rates.
19. **Quick Notes:** Add predefined notes to invoices (e.g., "Fragile", "Deliver after 5 PM").
20. **Price Check Mode:** A simple screen to scan a barcode and see the price without adding to invoice.

## 📦 Inventory & Products
21. **Product Variants:** Support sizes, colors, and flavors for a single product entry.
22. **Bundles/Kits:** Create combo products (e.g., "Shampoo + Conditioner") that deduct multiple stock items.
23. **Barcode Generation:** Built-in tool to generate and print barcode labels for products.
24. **Expiry Tracking:** Track batch numbers and expiry dates for perishable goods.
25. **Stock Transfer:** Manage inventory movement between different store branches or warehouses.
26. **Supplier Database:** Manage supplier contacts and link products to suppliers.
27. **Purchase Orders:** Generate PDF purchase orders to replenish stock automatically.
28. **Stocktake/Audit:** A mode for physical inventory counting to reconcile system stock vs. real stock.
29. **Low Stock Email:** Send automated emails to admin when items reach critical levels.
30. **Product Images:** Allow uploading images for products to display in the grid view.

## 👥 Customers & CRM
31. **Loyalty Points:** Earn points per currency spent, redeemable for discounts.
32. **Customer Tiers:** Auto-assign levels (Bronze, Silver, Gold) based on total spend.
33. **Birthday Rewards:** Auto-alert or discount on customer's birthday.
34. **Customer Wallet:** Allow customers to deposit money (Prepaid credit) to use later.
35. **Family Accounts:** Link multiple customers under one main account.
36. **Purchase History:** View "Frequently Bought" items for each customer to upsell.
37. **Customer Photos:** Webcam integration to take customer profile pictures.
38. **SMS Campaigns:** Send bulk SMS for offers (integration with Twilio/local gateways).
39. **Blacklist:** Flag bad customers to warn cashiers.
40. **Import/Export:** Bulk import customers from Excel/CSV.

## 📊 Reports & Analytics
41. **Z-Report (End of Day):** One-click report summarizing cash, card, and total sales for the shift.
42. **Profit/Loss Analysis:** Calculate profit based on Cost Price vs. Selling Price.
43. **Heatmap:** Visual representation of busiest hours/days.
44. **Employee Performance:** Track sales per cashier to calculate commissions.
45. **Top/Bottom Sellers:** Identify best-selling and worst-selling items.
46. **Tax Reports:** Detailed VAT/Tax collected reports for accounting.
47. **Email Reports:** Auto-email daily or weekly summaries to the owner.
48. **Expense Tracking:** Module to log shop expenses (Rent, Electricity, Tea/Coffee).
49. **Audit Log:** Track every action (who deleted an invoice? who changed a price?).
50. **Comparison Charts:** Compare this month's performance vs. last month.

## 🔧 Technical & Hardware
51. **Offline Sync:** (Bonus) If cloud-based, cache data locally and sync when internet returns.
52. **Customer Display:** Support a secondary screen facing the customer to show cart total.
53. **Weight Scale Integration:** Read weight directly from connected digital scales.
54. **Cash Drawer Trigger:** Signal to open cash drawer automatically after print.
55. **Self-Checkout Mode:** A simplified interface for customers to scan and pay themselves.
